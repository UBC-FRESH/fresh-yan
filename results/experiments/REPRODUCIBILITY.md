# Records reproducibility

All records under `results/` are regenerated by tracked commands (run from the
repository root). Every record carries `fy_commit`, `fy_version`, `ws3_version`, and
`libcbm_version` provenance columns.

## Carbon yield curves (`curves/`)

Managed (transition) curves are generated by **simulating the clearcut** in libCBM:
the stand grows on its natural trajectory to the model's typical rotation age, a harvest
event transfers the pools per CBM's disturbance matrix (age-0 conservation-consistent by
construction), and the stand regrows on the managed yield curve. The official curves use
the rotation-cycle equilibrium (`mode='cycle'`); natural curves are the spun-up
no-harvest trajectories (initial-landscape convention). Regenerate:

```bash
fresh-yan generate-curves --rotation-age 120
```

Rotation age 120 from measured area-weighted median harvest ages (110 base scenario;
120-125 emission-minimizing trade-off scenarios). See
`planning/curve-generation-and-reset-findings.md` for the defect this fixes (spun-up
age-0 states injected tens of tC/ha of phantom carbon at every harvest) and the
validation.

## No-harvest comparison (`results/no_harvest/`)

ws3's carbon yield-curve approach vs a direct libCBM run on the real TSA 24 landscape
(no harvest; model horizon). Regenerate:

```bash
fresh-yan no-harvest
```

Artifacts:

- `no_harvest_cbm.csv` — libCBM: per-period biomass, DOM, ecosystem stocks and net emission
- `no_harvest_ws3.csv` — ws3: same landscape with the carbon yield curves plugged in
- `no_harvest_pct_diff.csv` — period-wise percent difference (libCBM as benchmark)
- `no_harvest_pct_diff_summary.csv` — mean absolute percent difference per metric
- `no_harvest_carbon_stock_whole_st.pdf`, `no_harvest_carbon_dynamics_individual_st.pdf`

Biomass stocks reproduce the legacy `msc_walter_yan` committed outputs exactly; the
ws3-side DOM and ecosystem stocks differ by up to about 0.5% under the currently pinned
ws3 (the curve inputs are identical), with correspondingly small changes in net
emission. The records here add provenance columns and a tidy summary layout.

## Base scenario (`results/base_scenario/`)

Maximize even-flow harvest volume over the 200-year horizon (5% period-over-period
variation limits on harvested area and volume; the harvest action is flagged for
`cmp_c_z`). Regenerate:

```bash
fresh-yan base-scenario --solver highs
```

Artifacts: `base_scenario.csv` (per-period harvested area `oha`, harvested volume
`ohv`, growing stock `ogs`, ecosystem carbon `ocp`, emissions `ocf`; provenance columns
include the `solver`) and `base_scenario.pdf` (four-panel figure).

Solver note: HiGHS (open source, default) and Gurobi produce the same solution to
~1e-7; the manuscript's reported ranges (harvest area 369,000-408,000 ha/period;
harvest volume 7.45-8.23 x 10^7 m3/period; growing stock decreasing) reproduce with
either.

## Trade-off grid (`results/tradeoff_grid/`)

Emission-minimizing scenarios over the (displacement factor, solid-wood half-life) grid
(the inputs for the 3D/orthographic sensitivity figures): per-period harvest, HWP stock,
and emission trajectories plus a per-scenario summary. Regenerate:

```bash
fresh-yan trade-off-grid --workers 48
```

Accounting (corrected, `src/fresh_yan/accounting.py`): end-of-period stock-difference
ecosystem term; products-only HWP ledger (inflow = curve merchantable carbon x 0.85,
split 50/50 between paper and solid wood per the manuscript methods; survival per period
= 2**(-10/half-life)); substitution = displacement x solid-wood inflow x credit.
The LP objective equals exactly the negative of the reported tracker total, and the
telescoped conservation identity closes to zero. The published experiments used the
legacy flux-based accounting on the spun-up curves (start-of-period inventory values);
those results do not reproduce here by design -- see
`planning/curve-generation-and-reset-findings.md` for the corrected trade-off behavior
and the residual (~1% of landscape carbon). Verified 2026-10-11 (independent audit +
post-run checks): 5,000 rows, 25 x 200 unique, no NaN/inf; LP objective == -(tracker
total) spot cells exact (e.g. hl=100/df=1.5: 530,250,376.82 m3 utilized volume,
-1,586,066,278.9708 tCO2); worst net reset residual -20.19 MtC (1.63% of landscape C).
